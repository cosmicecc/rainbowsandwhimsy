About Us

Hi folks! We're a small, independent publishing company run by fantasy romance author Erica Hall.

Erica lives in beautiful, magnificent, glorious, and often rainy Scotland; ain't nowhere else she'd rather be (except maybe at the barrier of a gig for one of her faves! But even then, everyone knows Scottish gigs are the BEST! No Scotland No Party!!!) 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿

Some themes you will find in our writing:

  • Cosy, safe, warm hug vibes ☕
  • Found family (the ultimate trope!)
  • Finding and valuing your self-worth
  • Neurodivergence and queer positivity
  • Forests, castles, toadstools, and cute, charismatic creature friends 🍄
